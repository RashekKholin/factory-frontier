How to play

  1. Build mines on marked deposits.
  2. Connect materials using the link tool.
  3. Use smelters and assemblers to create circuits.
  4. Fulfill contracts to earn credits.
Connections transfer up to 4 units/sec within a three-tile range. Generator power is shared globally.

WELCOME TO YOUR FIRST FACTORY

Production is a puzzle.

Your goal is to produce circuits. Iron and copper mines supply smelters, while coal fuels the generator and smelters. The assembler combines iron plates and copper wire.

Place a building, select Link, then click the source machine and its destination. Materials flow along directed links.

New buildings need credits; complete the initial ore contract to grow your factory. Watch analytics to identify bottlenecks.
